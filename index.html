Important Announcement

We’re Moving to the New MyTrade!

Starting September 30, 2026, MyTrade is moving to a new and improved platform.

Your account, holdings, and relationship with MyTrade/Abacus stay exactly the same, only the platform changes.

⚠️ Important: GTC Orders

All existing GTC (Good-Til-Cancelled) orders will be cancelled as part of the transition. Please review and re-place any GTC orders you’d like to keep active on the new platform.

Everything else—your account, your portfolio, our support team—carries over with you.
